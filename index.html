  1. Install MacroDroid on your Vivo Y21.
  2. Trigger: Notification Received from GPay or Samsung Wallet containing ₹ or Paid.
  3. Action: Open Website with your deployed URL + ?auto=1&amount=150&mode=GPay&merchant=UPI.
  4. Set Vivo Y21 Battery mode for Chrome & MacroDroid to Unrestricted.
